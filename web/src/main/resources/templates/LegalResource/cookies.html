Cookie Notice

Last updated {legal.updated}

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. GiftLoop uses only a few, and only to keep you signed in and protect your account. We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and no other companies set cookies through GiftLoop.

Cookies we use

All of these are strictly necessary: GiftLoop can't work without them, so they don't need your consent. They're set by GiftLoop itself, can't be read by scripts on the page, and are sent only over secure connections.

NameWhat it's forHow long it lasts
gl_atKeeps you signed in (your current sign-in pass)About 1 hour, renewed while you use GiftLoop
gl_rtRenews your sign-in so you don't have to sign in again each hour30 days, or until you sign out
gl_csrfProtects forms from being submitted by other websites2 hours

Other storage

GiftLoop doesn't use local storage, tracking pixels or similar technologies. Your browser may cache files like images and styles to load pages faster.

Your choices

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block GiftLoop's cookies, you won't be able to sign in. Signing out deletes the sign-in cookies.

If we ever add cookies that aren't strictly necessary, we'll update this notice and ask for your consent first.

See our Privacy Policy, or email {legal.contactEmail}.
