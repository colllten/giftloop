Privacy Policy

Last updated {legal.updated}

This policy explains what information GiftLoop collects, how it's used and shared, and the choices you have. GiftLoop is run by {legal.operator} ("we", "us"), who is responsible for your information (the "controller" under European data protection law). Questions or requests: {legal.contactEmail}.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account: your email address and password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form; we can't read them.
  • Profile: username, display name, and optionally your birthday (month and day only, never the year) and a profile photo. Photos are re-saved when uploaded, which removes hidden details such as location data.
  • Groups and lists: groups you create or join and your role in them, events, wishlists, and the gifts on them, including names, prices, quantities, links, notes and photos.
  • Claims: gifts you claim or give, how many you've bought, who you're buying with, and whether you've chosen to hide your name.
  • Secret Santa: exchanges you join, pairs a group owner or admin keeps from drawing each other, and who you were drawn to give a gift to.
  • Messages to us: anything you include when you email us.

Information collected automatically

  • Sign-in and security records: our authentication provider keeps records such as when you signed in, your IP address and browser details, to secure accounts and limit abuse.
  • Short-lived limit records: to stop abuse, we note when you enter a wrong join code (kept 15 minutes) and when you use auto-fill (kept 1 hour). We don't store the codes you tried or the links you looked up in these records.
  • Server logs: our servers log technical events, such as errors and actions like creating a group, linked to your account ID. Our hosting provider may also log IP addresses.
  • Cookies: only the ones needed to keep you signed in and protect forms. See our cookie notice.

Auto-fill: when you ask GiftLoop to fill in a gift from a link, our server, not your device, visits that page and copies its name, price, description and image. The website sees our server's request, not your IP address or cookies.

2. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • create and secure your account, and send sign-in codes and important service emails;
  • run GiftLoop: show your profile, groups, lists and claims to the right people, and keep claims hidden from wishlist owners until they're revealed;
  • prevent abuse, fix problems and keep GiftLoop secure;
  • respond to your requests, and comply with the law.

We don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similar effects.

Legal bases (EU/UK users): we process your information to provide the service you asked for under our Terms (contract); to keep GiftLoop secure and prevent abuse (our legitimate interests); with your consent for optional details like your birthday and photo, which you can remove at any time; and where the law requires it.

3. Who can see what

  • Your profile (username, display name, birthday, photo) is visible to you and to people who share a group with you. Any signed-in user can check whether a username is already taken.
  • Your wishlists are visible to members of the group the event belongs to. You can choose to show a gift only to certain members.
  • Claims are visible to other members of the group, but not to the wishlist owner until the day after the event ends, or earlier if a group owner or admin turns that on. If you hide your name on a claim, only the people on that claim can see it was you.
  • Secret Santa: members of the group can see who joined an exchange. Only group owners and admins can see the pairs kept from drawing each other. Only you can see who you were drawn to give to; no one, including owners and admins, can see who is giving to you.
  • Group owners and admins can see the group's join code and manage members and events, but they can't see claims that are hidden from them as wishlist owners, Secret Santa draws, or your email address.
  • Other users can't see your email address.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run GiftLoop, under agreements that limit their use of it:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, file storage and accounts, and sends sign-in emails;
  • hosting and email delivery providers that run our servers and deliver emails.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or as part of transferring GiftLoop to a new operator, who would have to follow this policy. We don't sell or "share" personal information as those terms are defined by California law, and we never have.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account. When you do:

  • your profile, photo, group memberships and account are deleted;
  • groups you own pass to another member (you can choose who); groups where you're the only member are deleted;
  • your wishlists are removed, but gifts someone already claimed stay on their claims list, labeled as removed by a "Former member", until they release them;
  • claims you share pass to a co-buyer, and claims only you made are released;
  • you're removed from Secret Santa exchanges, along with any draw you were part of (an admin can draw again);
  • your sign-in sessions are ended, though a session open on another device may last up to an hour.

Copies may remain for a limited time in backups and provider logs before they're deleted on those systems' normal schedules, and some gift photos may take longer to be cleaned up. We may keep information longer if the law requires it. Content you delete yourself, like a gift or wishlist, is removed the same way.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: view and edit your profile at any time on your profile page.
  • Delete: delete your account yourself, or ask us to.
  • Get a copy: email us to request a copy of your information.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights:

  • EU, UK and similar laws: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on our legitimate interests, and withdrawing consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California and other US states: to know what we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and not to be treated differently for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information, or use sensitive information to infer things about you. If we deny your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer.

To make a request, email {legal.contactEmail} from the address on your account. We may need to confirm it's you, and we'll respond within the time the law requires (usually 30 to 45 days). You can use an authorized agent where the law allows.

7. Children

GiftLoop isn't for children under 13, and they may not create an account. Users under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal information, we'll delete it. If you think this has happened, contact us.

8. Where your information is stored

GiftLoop is run from the United States, and our providers may store information in the United States or other countries. If you use GiftLoop from outside the US, your information will be transferred there. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

9. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access rules in our database that limit each person to what they're allowed to see, and secure cookies. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password. If a breach affects you, we'll let you know as the law requires.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this policy when GiftLoop changes how it handles information. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version. If we make a significant change, we'll ask you to review it the next time you use GiftLoop.
