Wishlists your whole family can share

Make a wishlist for a birthday, a holiday or a Secret Santa. Everyone else claims what they'll buy, so nobody buys the same gift twice, and you won't see who's getting what until after the event.

Create an account Sign in

Someone sent you a join code? Join their group.

How it works

  1. Start a group

    Create a group for your family or friends, then share its join code.

  2. Make your wishlist

    Add gifts for an event with prices, links and photos. Paste a store link to fill in the details.

  3. Claim a gift

    Claim what you'll buy on someone else's list. Everyone sees it's taken, and you can split it with co-buyers.

Made for gift-giving

  • The surprise stays a surprise

    You can't see who claimed gifts on your list until the day after the event, unless a group owner or admin shows them early.

  • Secret Santa built in

    Draw names, set a budget and keep partners from drawing each other. Each person sees only who they're buying for.

  • Gifts that aren't on the list

    Buying something else? Record it on their list so others know it's covered. They see it after the event, like any claim.

  • Private to your group

    Only people in your group see your wishlists, and you can limit a gift to the people you pick.
